# KeeperCam Support

KeeperCam identifies a fish from a photo on your iPhone and shows the Florida
FWC size, bag and season limits for it, then keeps a log of your catches.

## Common questions

**The app got the species wrong.**
Tap **Adjust** on the result screen and draw a box around the fish, or pick a
different species from the list under the top guess. Identification is done on
your phone and works best with the whole fish in frame, side-on, in daylight.

**Are the rules up to date?**
The rule book is downloaded from the FWC regulations pages and refreshed in the
background. Rules can change at short notice, and KeeperCam is not affiliated
with FWC, so always confirm at [MyFWC.com](https://myfwc.com/fishing/saltwater/recreational/)
before keeping a fish.

**Where are my photos stored?**
On your phone only. Nothing you photograph is uploaded.

**How do I turn location tagging on or off?**
Account tab › "Tag my catches with where I caught them". It is off by default.

**How do I delete my account?**
Account tab › **Delete account**. Your catches stay on the phone.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen enter your email and tap **Forgot password?**. The email
contains a link that opens KeeperCam so you can choose a new one.
