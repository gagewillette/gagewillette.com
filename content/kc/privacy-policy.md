# KeeperCam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

KeeperCam is an iPhone app that identifies a fish from a photo and shows the
Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC) size, bag and season
limits for it. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- Photos never leave your phone. Species identification runs on the device.
- Your catch log, notes, measurements and any location pins stay on your phone.
- An account is optional. If you create one we store your email address, the
  name you give us, and a push-notification token so we can tell you when FWC
  changes a rule.
- We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics SDKs.
- You can delete your account, and everything we hold about it, from inside
  the app at any time.

## Information stored on your phone only

- **Photos and catches.** Pictures you take or choose, the species the app
  identified, lengths, notes and the date are saved in the app's own storage on
  your device. They are included in your iCloud or iTunes device backup like
  any other app data, and removed when you delete the app.
- **Location pins.** Location tagging is off by default. If you turn it on,
  the app asks iOS for permission and stores a coordinate with each catch so
  the log can show where it was caught and the rule book can pick the right
  coast. Locations are never sent to our servers.
- **Profile and settings.** Your display name, home waters, favourite species
  and the app's switches are stored locally.

## Information we receive if you create an account

Accounts are provided through Supabase (Supabase Inc., servers in the United
States).

- **Sign in with Apple.** We receive the identifier Apple assigns to you and,
  if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which may be an
  Apple relay address).
- **Email sign-up.** We receive the email address and password you enter. The
  password is stored only as a salted hash.
- **Rule-change alerts.** If you turn on alerts, iOS gives the app a push
  token that we store, together with the app version, your device language,
  and whether the build is a TestFlight/App Store build. We use it only to
  send a notification when the FWC rule book changes.

We use this information to sign you in, to send rule-change notifications you
asked for, and to respond if you contact us. We do not use it for marketing.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect your photos, catch log, locations, contacts, advertising
identifier, or precise device identifiers. The app contains no third-party
advertising or analytics code.

## Deleting your information

Open the **Account** tab and tap **Delete account**. This removes your account,
the email and name attached to it, and any push token, from our servers
immediately. Catches and photos on your phone are not touched; delete the app
to remove them.

Turning **Rule Change Alerts** off removes your push token from our servers.

## Children

KeeperCam is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, for material
changes, tell you inside the app.
